# Privacy Policy

_Draft template. Have it reviewed before you rely on it. Last updated: 2026-11-17._

## What we collect

- **Account data:** your email address, display name and preferred language.
- **Content you create:** CV entries, translations, sections, CVs and photos.
- **Rendered PDFs and thumbnails** generated from your CVs.
- **Messages you send** through the contact form.
- **Technical data** needed to run the service, such as server logs and rate-limit counters.

## Why we use it

To provide the service: sign you in, store your CVs, render PDFs, send the
emails you ask for (verification, password reset, render-ready notices) and
answer your messages.

## Who sees it

Only you, unless you publish a public profile or share a PDF. We use service
providers for hosting, storage and transactional email; they process data on our
behalf and only for those purposes. We do not sell your data.

## Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app. From Settings you can **export**
everything as JSON and **permanently delete** your account and all its data.
If you are in the EU or UK you also have the rights of access, correction,
erasure, portability and objection. Use the [contact form](/contact/) for
anything the app does not cover.

## Retention

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account removes it from
our database and storage; backups expire on their normal schedule.

## Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic uses HTTPS, and two-factor authentication is available.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change.
