# Cookie Policy

_Draft template. Have it reviewed before you rely on it. Last updated: 2026-11-17._

CVMaker uses only the cookies it needs to work. We do not use advertising or
cross-site tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss.

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sessionid` | Keeps you signed in | Session / up to 2 weeks |
| `csrftoken` | Protects forms from cross-site request forgery | About 1 year |

Your browser may also store small preferences (for example light or dark theme)
in local storage. These never leave your device.

If we add analytics later, we will update this page and ask for consent first
where the law requires it.
