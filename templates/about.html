About CVMaker

CVMaker keeps your career as reusable building blocks: entries (a job, a degree, a publication), grouped into sections, assembled into as many CVs as you need. Each entry can carry translations, so one CV renders in any language you have written for.

PDFs are produced by rendercv and Typst. Your data is yours: export or delete it any time from Settings.
