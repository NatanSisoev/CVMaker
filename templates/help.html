How CVMaker works

Write each piece of your experience once, then assemble CVs from them in any language and style.

The three building blocks

  1. 1. Entries

    One item: a job, a degree, a publication, a bullet point, a line of skills. Entries are reusable and can carry translations.

  2. 2. Sections

    A titled group of entries (Experience, Education, Publications). A section holds one kind of entry, and the same section can appear on several CVs.

  3. 3. CVs

    A CV lists sections in an order, plus your personal details and a style. You render it to a PDF in any language you have entries for.

Quick start

  1. Click + New CV on your dashboard and name it. The editor opens.
  2. Open Personal details and check your name and contact info.
  3. Click + Add section (for example "Experience").
  4. Inside the section, click + New entry, choose the kind and fill it in. Repeat.
  5. Pick a language and style, then Render preview. Download the PDF when you like it.

In the editor

  • Drag the ⠿ handles to reorder sections and the entries inside them (render again to see it in the PDF).
  • Click an entry to edit it in place. Under Translations, add a language (for example es) and fill the translated fields next to the original text. A blank field falls back to the original.
  • The language chips show how complete each translation is: filled, half-filled, or empty.
  • Drag the divider between the panes to resize them.
  • Press Ctrl/⌘ + K to jump to any CV, section or entry.

Good to know

  • One kind per section. A section of jobs can't also hold bullets. Make a second section instead.
  • Already have a CV? Import a rendercv YAML file.
  • Your data is yours. Download everything as JSON, or delete your account, from Settings.
  • Scripts and the CLI. Create an API token in Settings and see the API schema.
